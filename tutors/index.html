What is Atlas?

Atlas is a tutor-led workspace for adult language lessons. It brings structured conversation, spontaneous activities, learner continuity, and tutor control into one connected system.

The system

Atlas

The home and connective layer. Start with a learner, continue where you left off, and move between teaching worlds.

Compass

Guided conversation subjects built around ideas, perspectives, questions, and real discussion.

Arcade

Fast conversation games for spontaneity, energy, unexpected choices, and lighter lesson moments.

Using Atlas

01

Choose a learner — or skip it when you just want to get moving.

02

Choose Compass or Arcade depending on where the lesson needs to go.

03

Teach normally. Move around, adapt what you need, and return whenever the conversation takes you elsewhere.

The important part

You do not need to learn Atlas before you use it. Start teaching first. The deeper tools are there when you want them.

Use it

Teaching with Atlas

Atlas is designed to sit inside the lesson rather than dictate it. Choose a direction, follow the conversation, and move whenever the learner or the lesson gives you a reason to.

A lesson can move

Start

Pick up where you left off or choose something that fits the learner and the moment.

Explore

Use Compass when you want a deeper conversational direction or Arcade when the lesson needs something faster and looser.

Move

Leave, return, change direction, or adapt the material. The lesson remains yours.

Own it

Make it yours

Atlas does not treat its material as something you must preserve. Change what helps, keep what works, and create your own when the lesson calls for something different.

Different levels of control

01

Change content while you teach when something needs to work differently for the learner in front of you.

02

Keep your own version of an Atlas subject when your changes deserve to become part of how you teach it.

03

Create an independent subject when you want the material to become completely yours.

The principle

Atlas provides the starting point. Tutor judgement remains more important than the original material.

Pilot

Help shape Atlas

The pilot is about seeing what Atlas becomes when tutors actually teach with it — what helps, what gets in the way, what surprises you, and what you find yourself wishing existed.

What we want from you

01

Teach normally. You are not being asked to behave like a product tester during the lesson.

02

Notice the moments when Atlas genuinely improves the lesson — and the moments when it does not.

03

Tell us what you changed, ignored, wanted, misunderstood, or found unexpectedly useful.

Direction

What’s next

The pilot is the beginning of Atlas, not the finished product. What comes next will be shaped by what tutors actually need once the current system is in their hands.

Beyond the pilot

More ownership

Stronger tools for creating, adapting, organising, and reusing tutor-owned material.

Smarter creation

AI-assisted creation that works inside the teaching system rather than producing disconnected lesson material.

New formats

More flexible ways to assemble ideas, questions, media, and activities beyond the current structured subjects.

Not promises

This is direction rather than a fixed feature list. What proves useful in the pilot should determine what gets built.
