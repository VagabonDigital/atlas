What counts as a creation?

A fresh subject uses one creation when Atlas finishes creating it. If creation doesn’t complete, it doesn’t use your allowance. Editing, duplicating, reshaping and reusing existing material do not spend another fresh creation.

How do the two allowances work?

Atlas Free includes 8 fresh subject creations for the lifetime of your account. They do not expire with time. Atlas Pro includes 100 fresh subject creations each billing month. Your unused Free allowance is preserved while Pro is active.

What happens if I cancel Pro?

Your Pro access continues through the paid billing period, then your account returns to Free. Your saved teaching material and learner continuity remain available, along with any unused part of your lifetime Free allowance.

What stays included with Free?

Unlimited learners, Compass subjects and Atlas Originals, Arcade games, your saved library, learner and lesson continuity, and editing, shaping, duplicating and reusing your material. Portable export of supported account data is included too. Free is not a timed trial, and no card is required.

How is Pro billed?

Atlas Pro is a monthly subscription at $12 USD per month. Where available, the price above is localized for you. Any applicable taxes are shown at checkout. You can manage your subscription from your Atlas account.
