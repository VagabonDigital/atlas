A few useful answers

Pricing without the small-print maze.

What counts as one subject creation?

Each successfully created fresh subject uses one creation. Failed, incomplete or malformed attempts do not use the allowance.

Does changing an existing subject use another creation?

No. Manual editing, duplication and reshaping an existing subject do not spend another fresh subject creation.

Does Atlas Free expire?

No. Atlas Free is not a timed trial. Your lifetime bank of eight successful fresh subject creations does not expire simply because time passes.

What happens if I cancel Pro?

Your Pro access continues through the paid billing period, then the account returns to Free. Your saved workspace remains, and any unused part of your lifetime Free creation bank becomes available again.

Can I use Atlas with as many learners as I want?

Yes. Atlas does not use learner limits to separate Free and Pro.

Are Compass and Arcade only for Pro?

No. Atlas is designed so the teaching workspace remains useful on Free. Pro expands fresh subject creation rather than turning the rest of Atlas into locked scenery.
