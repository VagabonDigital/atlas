Refund & Cancellation Policy

A clear guide to cancelling Atlas Pro, requesting a refund, and what happens to your workspace when paid access ends.

Last updated: September 21, 2026

1. Scope

This policy applies to paid Atlas subscriptions purchased through Atlas. It should be read together with the Terms of Service and any purchase conditions shown at checkout.

Paddle acts as Merchant of Record for Atlas purchases. This means Paddle processes the payment, applicable transaction taxes, receipts, subscription billing, cancellations, and approved refunds for the transaction.

2. Cancelling Atlas Pro

You can cancel an Atlas Pro subscription at any time through the subscription-management link provided by Paddle. Cancellation stops future renewals and normally takes effect at the end of the billing period you have already paid for.

Unless a refund or other adjustment applies, Pro access continues until that paid period ends. Afterward, your Atlas account returns to the Free plan.

Your workspace is not deleted when you cancel.

Tutor-owned subjects, learner continuity, saved teaching material, manual editing, reuse, and other capabilities included in the Free plan remain available after a paid subscription ends.

3. Refund requests

Cancelling a subscription does not automatically refund the current billing period. If you want to request a refund, you can use the support or refund link provided by Paddle in your transaction receipt or billing portal, or contact support@atlasfortutors.com and we will help route the request.

Refund eligibility is assessed under Paddle’s applicable buyer terms and refund policy, together with any mandatory consumer rights that apply to you. Nothing in this policy limits rights that cannot lawfully be excluded or waived.

Where appropriate, Atlas may support a full or partial refund request for circumstances such as duplicate billing, an incorrect charge, or a material service problem. Paddle remains responsible for processing the transaction refund as Merchant of Record.

4. What happens after a refund

A full refund may end or adjust the paid entitlement associated with the refunded billing period. If paid access ends, the account returns to the Free plan rather than losing its saved workspace.

A refund and a subscription cancellation are separate actions. If you do not want future renewals, make sure the subscription is also cancelled.

5. Failed or disputed payments

If a renewal payment fails, Paddle may retry the payment or ask you to update your payment method. Atlas may temporarily preserve or restrict Pro access while the payment is being resolved according to the billing state shown by Paddle.

If a payment is reversed, charged back, refunded, or otherwise no longer supports an active paid period, Atlas may adjust the corresponding Pro entitlement. This does not remove non-paid workspace data that remains available under the Free plan.

6. Processing times

Approved refunds are returned through Paddle using the original payment method where possible. The time before funds appear can depend on the payment method, bank, card network, and Paddle’s processing timelines.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Atlas, its subscription model, or applicable payment requirements change. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date shown above and, where appropriate, by providing additional notice.

8. Contact

Questions about Atlas billing, cancellations, or refunds can be sent to support@atlasfortutors.com.
