Shuffling the deck…

A game for two voices

Find another way.

One of you describes. The other guesses out loud. Keep your call open in your lesson platform.

  1. Describe the target without saying it or any of the four forbidden words. Simple word endings count too.
  2. No spelling, letter clues, rhymes, translations, typing, drawing or deliberate miming. Normal gestures are fine.
  3. The Describer calls Correct for +1 shared point, Skip to move on, or I said one! after a forbidden word. No points are lost.
  4. Ask questions. Guess as often as you like. If a possible violation is unclear, keep playing.
  5. Clear cards for 60 seconds, then swap roles for another 60. Both turns make one round.

Ordinary forms of the answer count. Distinct derived words are allowed unless listed. If an answer arrived before the horn, you can credit that last card afterward.
