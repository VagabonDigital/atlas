Joining your partner… Opening the shared table.

A game for two voices

Find another way.

Keep your lesson call open. Describe the target without saying it or the four forbidden words. Ordinary word endings also count.

No spelling, letters, rhymes, translation, typed clues, drawing or deliberate miming. Normal gestures are fine.

Guess aloud as often as you like and ask questions. The Describer presses Correct (+1), Skip (0), or I said one! (0). If a violation is unclear, keep playing. Keyboard shortcuts: 1 = Correct · 2 = Skip · 3 = I said one.

You each describe for 2 minutes. The timer keeps running between cards. Together, your two turns make a round.
