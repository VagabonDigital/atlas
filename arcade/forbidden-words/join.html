A game for two voices

Find another way.

One of you describes the card. The other guesses aloud. You're a team, so keep your lesson call open and talk your way through the deck.

  1. Describe, don't name it. Don't say the target or any of the four forbidden words. Ordinary word endings count as the same word.
  2. Get creative with your clues. No spelling, letter hints, rhymes, translation, typing, drawing or deliberate miming. Normal gestures are fine.
  3. Keep the conversation going. Ask questions and guess as often as you like. If a possible rule break isn't clear, keep playing.
  4. Move on when you're ready. The Describer chooses Correct (+1 shared point), Skip (0), or I said one! (0) after using a forbidden word. You never lose points.Keyboard: 1 Correct · 2 Skip · 3 I said one!
  5. Swap after two minutes. The clock runs between cards. Then change roles for another two-minute turn. Both turns make one round.

Small but useful: Different derived words are allowed unless they're listed. If your partner guessed just before the buzzer, you can still credit that last card afterward.
