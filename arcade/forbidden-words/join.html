Joining your partner…

A game for two voices

Find another way.

Keep your lesson call open. Describe the target without saying it or the four forbidden words. Ordinary word endings also count.

No spelling, letters, rhymes, translation, typed clues, drawing or deliberate miming. Normal gestures are fine.

Guess aloud as often as you like and ask questions. The Describer presses Correct (+1), Skip (0), or I said one! (0). If a violation is unclear, keep playing.

You each describe for 60 seconds. The timer keeps running between cards. Together, your two turns make a round.
